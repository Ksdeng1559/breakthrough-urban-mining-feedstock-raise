# Feedstock Pipeline Stages

## Stage 1 - Target Identified
Potential feedstock source identified.

## Stage 2 - Research Complete
Decision makers, locations, material categories, and relationship paths documented.

## Stage 3 - Contacted
Initial outreach completed.

## Stage 4 - Discovery Call
Material profile discussion scheduled.

## Stage 5 - Material Assessment
Inventory and feedstock profile reviewed.

## Stage 6 - Sample Review
Material samples or supporting documentation reviewed.

## Stage 7 - Commercial Discussion
Pricing and logistics discussed.

## Stage 8 - LOI Drafted
Letter of Intent prepared.

## Stage 9 - LOI Signed
Supply relationship documented.

## Stage 10 - First Shipment
Initial material movement begins.

## Stage 11 - Active Supplier
Recurring feedstock source established.

## Stage 12 - Strategic Partner
Expanded commercial relationship.

## KPIs

- supplier conversations
- LOIs drafted
- LOIs signed
- tonnes secured
- PCB volume secured
- annualized feedstock value
- recurring suppliers
