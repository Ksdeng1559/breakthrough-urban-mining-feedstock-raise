# Investor Pipeline Stages

## Stage 1 - Sourced
Target identified.

## Stage 2 - Researched
Organization, decision maker, investment focus, and relationship paths documented.

## Stage 3 - Contacted
Initial outreach completed.

## Stage 4 - Engaged
Positive response received.

## Stage 5 - Discovery Call Scheduled
Meeting booked.

## Stage 6 - Discovery Completed
Investment fit confirmed.

## Stage 7 - NDA Sent
Confidentiality process initiated.

## Stage 8 - Data Room Access
Investor receives diligence materials.

## Stage 9 - Diligence Active
Questions, reviews, and follow-up discussions underway.

## Stage 10 - Site Visit / Facility Visit
Optional in-person validation event.

## Stage 11 - Subscription Package Sent
Legal documents distributed.

## Stage 12 - Soft Circle
Verbal commitment received.

## Stage 13 - Funded
Capital received.

## Stage 14 - Investor Relations
Ongoing reporting and updates.

## KPIs

- discovery calls
- data room access requests
- diligence participation
- soft circles
- funded commitments
- average commitment size
