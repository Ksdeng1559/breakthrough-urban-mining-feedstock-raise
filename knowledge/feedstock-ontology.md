# Feedstock Ontology

## Definition

Feedstock is material that can be acquired, processed, dismantled, refined, or otherwise converted into recoverable economic value.

For Breakthrough Urban Mining / MineTeck, feedstock primarily refers to enterprise and industrial electronic material containing recoverable metals such as gold, silver, copper, palladium, aluminum, and steel.

---

## Primary Feedstock Categories

### 1. Server Feedstock

Examples:

- Rack servers
- Blade servers
- Server motherboards
- Storage arrays
- Power supplies
- Copper cabling

Potential value drivers:

- Server boards
- Copper wiring
- Memory modules
- CPUs
- Power supplies

---

### 2. Telecom Feedstock

Examples:

- Telecom switching boards
- Carrier equipment
- Routers
- Switches
- Nortel-era boards
- Cisco and Juniper infrastructure

Potential value drivers:

- High-grade circuit boards
- Precious metal content
- Copper-rich components
- Recurring upgrade cycles

---

### 3. Networking Feedstock

Examples:

- Switches
- Routers
- Firewalls
- Network appliances
- Data center network hardware

Potential value drivers:

- Boards
- Copper ports
- Power supplies
- Enterprise-grade components

---

### 4. Industrial PCB Feedstock

Examples:

- PLCs
- SCADA boards
- Industrial controls
- Automation boards
- Mining and manufacturing electronics

Potential value drivers:

- Older board generations
- Specialized industrial components
- Higher board value per unit in some cases

---

### 5. Storage Feedstock

Examples:

- SAN systems
- NAS units
- Disk arrays
- Enterprise storage cabinets

Potential value drivers:

- Boards
- Drives
- Power supplies
- Aluminum and steel chassis

---

### 6. Mixed Enterprise Electronics

Examples:

- Desktops
- Workstations
- Office IT hardware
- UPS units
- Peripherals

Potential value drivers:

- Volume
- Copper
- Reusable components
- Relationship access

Caution:

Mixed electronics may be lower margin and more labour intensive unless acquired at the right price or in significant volume.

---

## Feedstock Quality Levels

### High Grade

Characteristics:

- Telecom boards
- Dense enterprise PCBs
- Industrial control boards
- High-value networking infrastructure

### Medium Grade

Characteristics:

- Server boards
- Storage systems
- Enterprise networking equipment

### Low Grade

Characteristics:

- Mixed office electronics
- Consumer equipment
- High steel / low board content material
