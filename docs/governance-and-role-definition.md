# Governance and Role Definition

## Purpose

This document defines the governance structure, decision rights, responsibilities, and review process for the Breakthrough Urban Mining Feedstock Raise initiative.

The objective is to ensure clear accountability during the 180-day GTM campaign and subsequent capital formation activities.

---

# Decision Framework

Major decisions should be evaluated using:

1. Strategic impact
2. Feedstock impact
3. Capital formation impact
4. Operational feasibility
5. Risk profile
6. Timeline implications
