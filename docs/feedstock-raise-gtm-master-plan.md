# Feedstock Raise GTM Master Plan

## Purpose

This document defines the go-to-market strategy for Breakthrough Management's $5M feedstock acquisition raise.

The immediate purpose of the raise is to secure high-value PCB, retired server, telecom, and electronics feedstock for Urban Mining / IMG precious metals recovery.

## Core Thesis

In conventional mining, reserves determine value.

In urban mining, secured feedstock determines value.

The opportunity is to control the ore body of the digital economy before the market reprices it.

## Primary Positioning

### Headline

Securing the Ore Body of the Digital Age

### Subheadline

Breakthrough Management is raising $5M to secure high-value printed circuit board and retired server feedstock for precious metals recovery.

## Investor Narrative

The global economy is creating increasing volumes of electronic waste through data center expansion, AI infrastructure, telecom upgrades, corporate IT refresh cycles, and consumer electronics turnover.

Printed circuit boards and retired server components contain recoverable gold, silver, palladium, platinum, copper, tin, and other metals.

The key constraint is not whether the metals exist. The key constraint is who controls the feedstock.
