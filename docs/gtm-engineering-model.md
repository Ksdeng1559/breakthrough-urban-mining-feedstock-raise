# GTM Engineering Model

## Purpose

This document defines the GTM Engineering model for Breakthrough Management's $5M Urban Mining feedstock acquisition raise.

The model adapts GTM Engineering principles to identify, score, and activate the accounts most likely to provide:

1. high-value PCB and server feedstock
2. strategic capital
3. family office capital
4. referral access
5. non-dilutive grant or program support

## Core Definition

GTM Engineering for Urban Mining means using market signals, company data, relationship intelligence, and automated workflows to identify who controls feedstock, who can fund acquisition, and who can become a strategic partner.

## Strategic Thesis

The raise should not rely on generic outbound activity.

It should rely on signal-driven targeting.

The system must detect who is likely to have feedstock, who is likely to care about the problem, who has capital, and who has a relationship path into the opportunity.

## RIOS Role

RIOS acts as the operating layer that connects signals, research, relationships, scoring, and execution.

### RIOS Modules

1. Signal Worker
2. Account Research Worker
3. Relationship Intelligence Worker
4. Fit Scoring Worker
5. Outreach Personalization Worker
6. Follow-Up Worker
7. Data Room Engagement Worker
8. LOI / Commitment Tracker

## Signal Worker

Responsibilities:

- detect new target accounts
- identify trigger events
- flag companies likely to control feedstock
- flag investors likely to fund feedstock acquisition
- route targets into the correct pipeline

## Account Research Worker

Responsibilities:

- summarize target company
- identify decision makers
- classify material type or investor type
- estimate potential value
- detect compliance or ESG language

## Relationship Intelligence Worker

Responsibilities:

- identify warm paths
- map LinkedIn connections
- identify advisors, attorneys, consultants, and alumni connections
- recommend best introduction path

## Fit Scoring Worker

Responsibilities:

- apply feedstock fit score
- apply investor fit score
- rank accounts
- recommend next action

## Outreach Personalization Worker

Responsibilities:

- generate segment-specific messaging
- insert account-specific signal
- match CTA to account type
- avoid generic cold outreach

## Strategic Conclusion

The highest-probability path to the first $5M is not volume-based outreach.

It is signal-based GTM Engineering.

The system must find where feedstock is likely to emerge, who controls it, who can fund it, and which relationship path can open the conversation.

The strongest proof point for investors will be signed feedstock LOIs.

Therefore, the GTM Engineering system should prioritize feedstock signals first and investor activation second.

Feedstock proof creates investor confidence.
Investor capital increases feedstock control.
Together, they create the operating flywheel for Urban Mining.
