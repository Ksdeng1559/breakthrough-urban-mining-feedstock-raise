# MineTeck / Breakthrough $1B Ecosystem Mind Map

## Purpose

This document maps the MineTeck / Breakthrough ecosystem as a capital formation, feedstock acquisition, grant funding, and operating platform designed to support the long-term objective of building a $1B+ resource recovery company.

The core idea is simple:

> In conventional mining, reserves determine value. In urban mining, secured feedstock determines value.

Feedstock is the ore body of urban mining.

## Strategic Narrative

The $5M feedstock raise is not only an operations raise. It is the capital required to secure the ore body of urban mining: PCB-rich, server-derived, telecom, and enterprise electronics feedstock.

RIOS is not just a CRM. RIOS is the signal intelligence layer that identifies and scores feedstock suppliers, investors, grant opportunities, strategic partners, and relationship paths.

Foundersuite is the capital relationship system of record. RIOS remains the signal and discovery layer.
