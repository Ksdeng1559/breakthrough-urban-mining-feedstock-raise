# 180-Day Execution Plan

## Purpose

This plan extends the Urban Mining feedstock raise execution window from 90 days to 180 days, with a formal review every 90 days.

The objective is to build a repeatable GTM system that secures feedstock LOIs, develops strategic investor interest, and supports the $5M feedstock acquisition raise.

## Review Cadence

- **Day 90 Review:** Validate campaign traction, feedstock LOI progress, investor engagement, and messaging performance.
- **Day 180 Review:** Evaluate capital raised, feedstock secured, strategic partnerships, and readiness for scale-up or revised execution.

# Day 90 Review Gate

## Review Questions

1. Which feedstock segments produced the highest-quality conversations?
2. Which investor segments responded best to the feedstock-first thesis?
3. How many supplier LOIs were signed or are realistically close?
4. How much capital has been soft-circled or committed?
5. Are investors asking for more operating proof, more supplier proof, or better economics?
6. Which outreach channel performed best?
7. Should messaging remain feedstock-first, or should strategic partnership framing be emphasized more?

## Day 90 Decision Options

### Option A - Scale

Use if:

- 5+ strong feedstock opportunities exist
- investor conversations are active
- data room engagement is meaningful

Action:

- Increase outreach volume
- add more verticals
- increase investor briefings
- pursue larger strategic investors

### Option B - Refine

Use if:

- some traction exists but conversion is slow
- messaging is working in some segments but not others

Action:

- refine ICPs
- narrow segments
- improve offer
- strengthen LOI language

### Option C - Reposition

Use if:

- investor interest is weak
- suppliers respond but investors do not
- capital raise thesis needs stronger proof

Action:

- focus 60 days primarily on supplier LOIs
- delay investor push
- build stronger proof before raising aggressively

# Day 180 Review Gate

## Review Questions

1. How much capital has closed?
2. How much capital is soft-circled?
3. How many signed feedstock LOIs or agreements exist?
4. What feedstock volume has been secured or forecasted?
5. Which supplier segments are most attractive?
6. Which investor segments are most attractive?
7. Does the $5M raise remain the right amount?
8. Should the next phase focus on investors, suppliers, strategic partners, or grants?
9. Is a dedicated feedstock acquisition vehicle needed?
10. Is the campaign ready for scale?

## Success Criteria

The campaign is successful if it demonstrates:

1. Investor confidence
2. Feedstock acquisition momentum
3. credible supplier LOIs or agreements
4. repeatable outreach economics
5. clear path to closing the full $5M raise

Feedstock LOIs are the primary proof point that strengthens capital formation.

## Strategic Principle

Do not scale capital solicitation faster than feedstock proof.

Feedstock proof creates investor confidence.
Investor capital increases feedstock control.
Together, they create the operating flywheel for Urban Mining.
