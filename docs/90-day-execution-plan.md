# 90-Day Execution Plan

## Days 1-15

### Build Infrastructure

- Investor one pager
- Feedstock brief
- CRM pipelines
- RIOS workspace
- Data room architecture
- Initial supplier list
- Initial investor list

### Targets

- 500 supplier accounts researched
- 250 investor accounts researched

## Days 16-30

### Supplier Outreach Launch

Focus:

- data centers
- ITAD firms
- telecom providers
- universities
- hospitals

### Targets

- 20 supplier conversations
- 5 active LOI discussions

## Days 31-45

### Investor Outreach Launch

Focus:

- strategic investors
- family offices
- resource investors

### Targets

- 15 investor conversations
- 5 data room requests

## Days 46-60

### Validation Stage

- secure feedstock LOIs
- create supplier dashboard
- investor updates
- strategic partner discussions

### Targets

- 3 to 5 feedstock LOIs
- 25 investor conversations
- $500K to $1M soft circle interest

## Days 61-75

### Conversion Stage

- investor briefings
- diligence meetings
- subscription package delivery

### Targets

- 1 to 3 committed investors
- 5 active supplier opportunities

## Days 76-90

### First Close Preparation

- close investors
- finalize supplier agreements
- publish campaign update
- prepare second outreach wave

### Targets

- $500K to $1.5M first close
- 5 to 10 feedstock opportunities

## Success Criteria

The campaign is successful if it demonstrates both:

1. Investor confidence
2. Feedstock acquisition momentum

Feedstock LOIs are the primary proof point that strengthens capital formation.
