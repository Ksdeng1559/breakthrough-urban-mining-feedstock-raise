# Investor One-Pager

## Securing the Ore Body of the Digital Age

Breakthrough Management is raising $5M to secure high-value printed circuit board, retired server, telecom, and electronic waste feedstock for precious metals recovery.

## The Core Thesis

In conventional mining, reserves determine value.

In urban mining, secured feedstock determines value.

Printed circuit boards and retired server infrastructure contain recoverable gold, silver, palladium, platinum, copper, tin, and other valuable metals. The opportunity is to secure high-quality feedstock before the market fully reprices these above-ground resources.

## Why Now

- AI and data center expansion are accelerating server deployment and refresh cycles.
- Corporate IT and telecom infrastructure continues to generate high-grade electronic waste.
- E-waste is one of the fastest-growing waste streams globally.
- Precious metals recovery from existing electronics reduces the need for new mining disturbance.
- Feedstock agreements create a stronger, more financeable operating model.
