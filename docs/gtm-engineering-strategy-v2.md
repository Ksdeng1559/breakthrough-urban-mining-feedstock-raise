# MineTeck Urban Mining Feedstock Intelligence Platform™
## GTM Engineering Strategy v2.0

## Vision

Build North America's first AI-powered Feedstock Intelligence Network that identifies, acquires, and secures electronic waste feedstock before it reaches traditional recyclers.

MineTeck's competitive advantage will not be processing technology alone.

The competitive advantage will be proprietary intelligence regarding where future feedstock originates.

---

## Strategic Thesis

Most recyclers compete for available feedstock.

MineTeck will identify feedstock before it becomes available.

This changes the business model from:

**Reactive Purchasing**

To:

**Predictive Feedstock Acquisition**

---

## Core GTM Engineering Architecture

### Layer 1 — Signal Intelligence

Purpose:

Identify organizations likely to generate feedstock.

Data sources:

- Explorium
- Apollo
- Apify
- Government databases
- Asset auction platforms
- Bankruptcy filings
- Construction permits
- Data center announcements
- Telecom infrastructure projects
- M&A activity

Output:

- Feedstock opportunity signals

---

### Layer 2 — Feedstock Intelligence Engine

Purpose:

Convert signals into opportunities.

Components:

#### Feedstock Worker

Identifies:

- Data center refreshes
- Hardware replacement cycles
- Network upgrades
- Facility closures
- Technology migrations

#### Opportunity Scoring Worker

Scores:

- Material volume
- Technology density
- Precious metal concentration
- Relationship accessibility
- Logistics complexity

Output:

- Priority-ranked opportunities

---

### Layer 3 — Printing Press Command Layer

Purpose:

Create reusable intelligence commands.

Example commands:

```bash
feedstock scan
feedstock score
feedstock enrich
facility monitor
auction scan
relationship map
investor search
supplier rank
```

This layer becomes the operating system for MineTeck intelligence operations.

Rather than manually researching opportunities, operators execute repeatable commands.

Example:

```bash
feedstock scan --industry datacenter --region canada
```

Output:

- Ranked feedstock opportunities

---
