# Avestor Evaluation

## Purpose

This document defines how Breakthrough Management should evaluate Avestor or a similar customizable fund platform as a digital investor experience layer for the Urban Mining feedstock raise.

The objective is to determine whether Avestor can support the existing paper-based, SEC-registered or counsel-approved fund structure without disrupting compliance.

---

# Evaluation Thesis

Avestor should be evaluated as a digital collateral, onboarding, reporting, and fund administration layer.

It should not be treated as a replacement for:

- securities counsel
- PPM
- subscription agreement
- accreditation process
- investor disclosures
- SEC or Reg D compliance requirements

---

# Key Questions for Avestor

## Fund Structure

1. Can Avestor support an existing paper-based fund structure?
2. Can it support a Reg D or SEC-registered private offering workflow?
3. Can it support one fund with multiple project pages or deal sleeves?
4. Can investors express allocation preferences by opportunity?
5. Can it support continuous fundraising?

## Investor Onboarding

1. Does the platform support digital onboarding?
2. Does it support investor questionnaires?
3. Does it integrate with accreditation verification providers?
4. Does it support KYC / AML workflows where needed?
5. Does it support DocuSign or built-in e-signature?

## Data Room and Documents

1. Can documents be gated by investor status?
2. Can documents be gated by NDA?
3. Can activity be tracked by investor?
4. Can documents be version controlled?
5. Can investor downloads be restricted or logged?

## Reporting

1. Can investors receive periodic updates?
2. Can reports be segmented by investment sleeve?
3. Can capital deployment be reported digitally?
4. Can operating milestones be displayed?
5. Can investor tax or administrative materials be hosted?

## CRM and RIOS Integration

1. Can investor activity be exported?
2. Does it integrate with HubSpot, Attio, or other CRMs?
3. Can RIOS ingest investor activity from the platform?
4. Can data room engagement trigger follow-up?
5. Can investor interest by sleeve be tracked?

## Compliance

1. What audit trails are maintained?
2. Can communications be archived?
3. Can disclaimers be displayed by page or document?
4. Can access be restricted until accreditation is confirmed?
5. Can securities counsel review platform-hosted materials before publication?

# Decision Options

## Option A - Use Avestor Directly

Best if:

- it supports the current legal structure
- counsel approves
- speed matters more than customization
- platform cost is acceptable

## Option B - Use Avestor as Reference Architecture

Best if:

- Breakthrough wants a custom RIOS-integrated investor portal later
- current raise can proceed with a simpler data room first
- Avestor economics or constraints are not ideal

## Option C - Hybrid

Use Avestor or another fund platform for administration while RIOS manages relationship intelligence, outreach, and engagement tracking.
