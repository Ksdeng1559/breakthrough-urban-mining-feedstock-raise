# Loom Personalized Video Strategy

## Purpose

This document defines how Breakthrough Management should use Loom videos as a personalized trust-building and conversion tool for investor prospects, strategic partners, feedstock suppliers, grant collaborators, and interested parties.

Loom should complement Pictory.

- Pictory = polished evergreen education
- Loom = personalized relationship follow-up

---

# Strategic Role of Loom

Loom should be used when a prospect has shown interest, has been referred, has replied, has opened materials, or requires a more personal explanation.

The goal is to make the conversation feel direct, human, and specific to the recipient.

Loom should not replace:

- legal documents
- investor qualification
- securities counsel review
- accreditation verification
- data room diligence
- subscription documents

---

# When to Use Loom

## Use Loom After

- warm introduction
- positive email reply
- LinkedIn response
- investor discovery call
- strategic partner conversation
- feedstock supplier conversation
- data room access request
- grant partner inquiry
- site visit or facility discussion

## Do Not Use Loom For

- mass cold outreach
- disclosing confidential materials publicly
- making unreviewed securities claims
- promising returns
- replacing the data room
- bypassing qualification or compliance steps

# Loom Video Types

## 1. Warm Introduction Loom

### Audience

- referred family office
- referred strategic investor
- referred supplier
- referred advisor

### Length

60 to 90 seconds

### Structure

1. Thank the referrer / acknowledge connection
2. Explain why the opportunity may be relevant
3. Name the specific angle that connects to the recipient
4. Suggest a short conversation

### Example Opening

Hi [First Name], Dennis here. [Referrer] suggested I reach out because of your background in [sector]. I wanted to make this personal rather than send a long deck.

# Recommended Investor Loom Script

## Title

Why I Thought This May Fit Your Investment Lens

## Script

Hi [First Name], Dennis here.

I wanted to send a quick personal note rather than another long document.

The reason I thought this may be relevant to you is that Breakthrough Management is developing an Urban Mining strategy built around a simple idea: in conventional mining, reserves determine value. In urban mining, secured feedstock determines value.

The immediate $5M raise is focused on securing PCB, retired server, telecom, and high-value e-waste feedstock. That feedstock is the input stream that supports the recovery economics.

What makes this different from a typical recycling story is that the opportunity is being approached as a resource recovery platform: feedstock acquisition, investor capital, strategic supplier relationships, and eventually AI and robotics automation for existing recycling facilities.

I thought this may fit your lens because of your background in [specific reason].

If useful, I would be glad to send the Feedstock Acquisition Brief or schedule a short call to see whether it is worth a deeper review.

Thanks, and I appreciate you taking a look.

# Best Practices

## Keep Looms Short

Most Looms should be 60 seconds to 3 minutes.

Only data room walkthroughs should be 3 to 5 minutes.

## Be Specific

The value of Loom is personalization.

Mention:

- the person's company
- their background
- the signal that made them relevant
- the specific next step

## Avoid Overproduction

Loom should feel personal, not scripted or corporate.

## Use Screen Share When Helpful

Best screen-share assets:

- investor one-pager
- feedstock acquisition brief
- target account logic
- data room map
- grant strategy overview

## Keep Compliance in Mind

For investor Looms:

- avoid guaranteed returns
- avoid unsupported projections
- avoid unapproved securities claims
- avoid confidential content before NDA
- point to counsel-reviewed documents for formal details

Pictory explains the opportunity.

Together, they create a more human and scalable capital formation workflow.
