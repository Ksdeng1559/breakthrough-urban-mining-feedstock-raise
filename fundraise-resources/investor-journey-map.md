# Investor Journey Map

## Stage 1 - Awareness

Investor discovers the opportunity through:

- referral
- family office network
- strategic partner introduction
- investor briefing
- conference
- outreach campaign

Deliverables:

- executive summary
- investor one-pager
- digital information page

## Stage 2 - Qualification

Investor is assessed for:

- investment fit
- accredited status
- strategic alignment
- interest level

Deliverables:

- qualification call
- CRM record
- next-step recommendation

## Stage 3 - Diligence

Investor reviews:

- data room
- governance
- feedstock pipeline
- use of funds
- grant strategy
- risks

Deliverables:

- NDA if required
- data room access
- diligence Q&A

## Stage 4 - Subscription

Investor completes:

- subscription package
- accreditation process
- fund transfer process

## Stage 5 - Reporting

Investor receives:

- capital deployment updates
- feedstock updates
- milestone updates
- review summaries

## KPI Tracking

- awareness to qualification
- qualification to diligence
- diligence to subscription
- subscription to repeat investment
