# Foundersuite Capital Formation CRM Strategy

## Purpose

Foundersuite is the capital formation CRM and investor relationship operating layer for the Breakthrough Urban Mining Feedstock Raise.

The platform should not be used as the primary feedstock sourcing system. Feedstock discovery, supplier qualification, and operational signal intelligence should remain inside the GTM Engineering / RIOS workflow.

Foundersuite is used to manage the capital-side relationships required to finance feedstock acquisition, working capital, strategic partnerships, diligence, and investor communications.

---

## Strategic Role

### Foundersuite Is Used For

- investor pipeline management
- family office relationship tracking
- strategic partner capital tracking
- data room organization
- investor update distribution
- warm introduction tracking
- diligence workflow management
- soft-circle and commitment tracking

### Foundersuite Is Not Used For

- feedstock discovery
- supplier scraping
- auction monitoring
- recycler prospecting
- government surplus sourcing
- operational feedstock qualification
- technical recovery analysis

---

## Capital Formation Positioning

The urban mining thesis depends on control of feedstock.

The $5M raise is designed to secure PCB, retired server, telecom, and enterprise electronics feedstock before the market fully reprices high-grade electronic waste streams.

Foundersuite supports this by creating a structured capital relationship system that connects:

```text
Feedstock Opportunity
↓
Capital Requirement
↓
Investor / Family Office / Strategic Partner Match
↓
Data Room Access
↓
Diligence
↓
Soft Circle / Commitment
↓
Feedstock Acquisition Capacity
```

## Data Room Structure Inside Foundersuite

The data room should support investor diligence without over-disclosing unreviewed or legally sensitive materials.

All investor-facing materials must be reviewed by qualified counsel before distribution.

### 1. Executive Materials

- executive summary
- investor one-pager
- investor deck
- use of proceeds
- 180-day execution plan
- management bios

### 2. Feedstock Thesis

- target feedstock categories
- PCB and retired server acquisition rationale
- supplier universe summary
- LOI status summary
- feedstock qualification framework
- chain-of-custody plan

### 3. Technical and Recovery Materials

- assay reports
- lab analysis
- recovery test summaries
- process workflow
- technical validation notes
- approved processing partner information

### 4. Commercial Materials

- target account universe
- supplier outreach strategy
- strategic partner pipeline summary
- buyer / refiner network summary
- revenue model
- market positioning

### 5. Financial Materials

- $5M use-of-proceeds model
- working capital plan
- feedstock acquisition model
- scenario model
- capital stack plan

### 6. Governance and Compliance

- governance and role definition
- compliance review checklist
- legal counsel review status
- securities disclaimer
- investor communication policy

RIOS remains the signal and relationship intelligence layer.

Foundersuite becomes the capital relationship system of record.

```text

↓

↓

```
