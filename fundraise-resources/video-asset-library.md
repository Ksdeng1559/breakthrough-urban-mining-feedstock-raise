# Video Asset Library

## Purpose

This document is the master catalog for all video assets used in the Urban Mining feedstock raise, supplier acquisition, strategic partner outreach, grant collaboration, and investor education workflows.

The video stack is:

- Pictory for polished evergreen education
- Vidyard for trackable video distribution and intent signals
- Loom for personalized trust-building and follow-up
- RIOS for relationship memory, scoring, and next-step routing

---

# Video Stack Summary

## Pictory

Use for reusable videos that explain the thesis.

Best for:

- investor education
- supplier education
- strategic partner education
- grant and R&D education
- data room introduction videos

## Vidyard

Use for trackable video campaigns.

Best for:

- campaign-level distribution
- watch analytics
- CTA tracking
- CRM / RIOS engagement scoring
- identifying high-intent prospects

## Loom

Use for personalized videos.

Best for:

- one-to-one investor follow-up
- post-meeting recaps
- data room walkthroughs
- personalized supplier outreach
- strategic partner follow-up

# Distribution Rules

## Cold or Semi-Warm Prospect

Use Vidyard with a short educational video.

Goal:

- capture watch behavior
- identify interest
- trigger follow-up

## High-Intent Prospect

Use Loom.

Goal:

- personalize message
- reference their background
- invite a specific next step

## Data Room Prospect

Use Loom walkthrough.

Goal:

- reduce friction
- explain what to review first
- clarify next step

## Website or Portal Visitor

Use Pictory embed or Vidyard embed.

Goal:

- explain the opportunity clearly
- route to qualification form
