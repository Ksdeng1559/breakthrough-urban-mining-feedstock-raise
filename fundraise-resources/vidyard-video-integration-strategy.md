# Vidyard Video Integration Strategy

## Purpose

This document defines how Breakthrough Management can evaluate and integrate Vidyard into the Urban Mining fundraising, feedstock acquisition, strategic partner, and grant collaboration workflows.

Vidyard should be considered the trackable video engagement layer for prospecting and investor relationship intelligence.

---

# Video Stack Positioning

## Pictory

Role: polished evergreen video presentations.

Best for:

- investor education videos
- supplier education videos
- grant / R&D explanation videos
- strategic partner overview videos
- website and data room explainers

## Loom

Role: personalized one-to-one follow-up.

Best for:

- warm introductions
- post-call recaps
- data room walkthroughs
- supplier-specific explanations
- investor-specific explanations

## Vidyard

Role: trackable sales video and engagement intelligence.

Best for:

- investor outreach campaigns
- supplier outreach campaigns
- strategic partner prospecting
- video analytics
- CRM-triggered follow-up
- team-shared video templates
- relationship scoring

---

# Strategic Role of Vidyard

Vidyard can help answer a key GTM question:

> Which prospects are actually engaging with the video thesis?

For the Urban Mining campaign, this matters because video engagement can become a buying signal or investor intent signal.

A prospect who watches 80% of a feedstock acquisition video should be treated differently than someone who never opens the email.

---

# Recommended Use Cases

## 1. Investor Outreach Video Tracking

Send investor prospects a short video explaining:

- feedstock is the ore body
- why the $5M raise exists
- what the capital is used for
- why feedstock LOIs matter

Track:

- video opened
- video watched
- watch percentage
- replay activity
- call-to-action clicks

RIOS action:

- high engagement = priority follow-up
- partial engagement = educational follow-up
- no engagement = alternate subject line or different angle

---

## 2. Feedstock Supplier Outreach

Send supplier prospects a short video explaining:

- why PCB-rich material is valuable
- why retired servers and telecom equipment matter
- how Urban Mining may create a better downstream recovery path
- how a supply conversation works

Track:

- watched video
- clicked CTA
- replied after video
- booked supplier call

RIOS action:

- high engagement = call request
- clicked but did not reply = send personalized Loom
- no engagement = send plain text follow-up

---

## 3. Strategic Partner Outreach

Use Vidyard to send a platform overview to:

- ITAD firms
- recyclers
- data center decommissioning companies
- industrial recyclers
- robotics partners
- AI/computer vision partners

Track:

- engagement by company
- stakeholder engagement across multiple contacts
- which partnership angle gets the most views

RIOS action:

- multi-contact engagement = account-level priority score increase

---

## 4. Data Room Pre-Qualification

Before granting deeper diligence access, send a short video:

- high-level thesis
- what is inside the data room
- what the investor should review first
- compliance reminder

Track:

- whether investor watched the introduction
- whether they clicked to request access
- whether follow-up is needed

---

## 5. Grant / R&D Partner Outreach

Send research partners and pilot facilities a video explaining:

- AI and robotics retrofit concept
- why existing recycling facilities need automation
- what collaboration could look like
- why letters of support matter

Track:

- interested labs or facilities
- clicks to schedule collaboration call
- response by partner type

---

# Vidyard vs Loom Decision Rule

Use Vidyard when the goal is:

- campaign distribution
- analytics
- CRM tracking
- scalable prospect education
- team-shared video assets

Use Loom when the goal is:

- highly personal follow-up
- direct relationship building
- post-meeting recap
- individual data room walkthrough

Use Pictory when the goal is:

- polished evergreen collateral
- cinematic story
- presentation-quality explainer

# RIOS Integration Model

## Vidyard Engagement Fields

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Vidyard Video Sent | Yes / No |
| Video Name | Name of video sent |
| Segment | Investor, supplier, partner, grant |
| Send Date | Date sent |
| Opened | Yes / No |
| Watch Percentage | 0-100% |
| CTA Clicked | Yes / No |
| Replay Count | Number of replays |
| Account-Level Views | Number of contacts at same company who watched |
| Engagement Score | Calculated score |
| Recommended Next Action | Loom, call, data room, LOI, nurture |

## Engagement Scoring

| Behavior | Score |
|---|---:|
| Opened video page | +5 |
| Watched 25% | +10 |
| Watched 50% | +20 |
| Watched 80%+ | +35 |
| Rewatched video | +15 |
| Clicked CTA | +30 |
| Forwarded / multiple company views | +25 |
| Replied after video | +40 |
| Booked call | +60 |

## Trigger Logic

### High Intent

Criteria:

- watched 80%+
- clicked CTA
- replayed video
- multiple contacts watched

Action:

- send personalized Loom
- request call
- assign to Andrew, Gilbert, or Dennis depending on account type

### Medium Intent

Criteria:

- watched 25-79%
- no CTA click

Action:

- send educational follow-up
- offer one-pager
- invite question

### Low Intent

Criteria:

- no watch or no open

Action:

- change subject line
- send shorter plain-text follow-up
- try different angle after 7-14 days

# Compliance Guardrails

Vidyard videos sent to investor prospects should be treated as investor-facing communications.

Before use, review for:

- unapproved return claims
- unsupported financial projections
- unsupported technical claims
- misleading ESG language
- offering-specific information shown before qualification
- missing disclaimers

Recommended safe framing:

- educational overview
- thesis explanation
- request for qualified conversation
- direct prospects to counsel-reviewed materials for formal offering details

Avoid:

- guaranteed returns
- risk-free language
- investment promises
- unverified environmental claims
- confidential data shown before NDA

# Strategic Conclusion

Vidyard should sit between Pictory and Loom.

Pictory creates polished reusable videos.

Vidyard distributes trackable sales videos and captures intent signals.

Loom personalizes high-intent follow-up.

RIOS stores the relationship intelligence and triggers the next action.

Together, the stack becomes:

```text
Pictory = create the core story
Vidyard = distribute and track engagement
Loom = personalize trust-building follow-up
RIOS = remember, score, and route the relationship
```
