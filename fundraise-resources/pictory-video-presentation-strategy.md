# Pictory Video Presentation Strategy

## Purpose

This document defines how Breakthrough Management can use Pictory video presentations to educate prospects, interested parties, strategic partners, and qualified investors about the Urban Mining feedstock acquisition opportunity.

The goal is to make complex ideas easier to understand before a discovery call, data room review, or investor briefing.

---

# Strategic Role of Video

Pictory should be used as a prospect education and relationship-warming tool.

It should not replace:

- securities counsel review
- investor qualification
- accreditation verification
- PPM or offering documents
- subscription documents
- formal diligence process

Video should help prospects understand the opportunity faster so that live conversations are more productive.

---

# Recommended Video Tracks

## Track 1 - Investor Education Video

### Purpose

Explain the $5M feedstock acquisition raise in a concise, visual way.

### Target Audience

- family offices
- strategic investors
- resource investors
- ESG / circular economy investors

### Suggested Length

3 to 5 minutes

### Core Message

In conventional mining, reserves determine value. In urban mining, secured feedstock determines value.

### CTA

Request the Feedstock Acquisition Brief or schedule a qualified investor call.

---

## Track 2 - Feedstock Supplier Video

### Purpose

Explain why PCB-rich and server-derived material should be treated as high-value feedstock rather than generic scrap.

### Target Audience

- ITAD firms
- data center operators
- telecom asset recovery teams
- enterprise IT departments
- institutional surplus managers

### Suggested Length

2 to 4 minutes

### Core Message

Urban Mining provides a pathway to convert retired electronics into a precious metals recovery stream with better chain-of-custody and circular economy reporting.

### CTA

Discuss current downstream path for PCB, server, and telecom hardware.

---

## Track 3 - Strategic Partner Video

### Purpose

Position Urban Mining as a platform that can combine feedstock, processing, AI/robotics R&D, and facility modernization.

### Target Audience

- recycling companies
- ITAD operators
- strategic investors
- data center decommissioning firms
- industrial recyclers

### Suggested Length

4 to 6 minutes

### Core Message

The opportunity is not only e-waste recovery. The opportunity is to build a circular economy infrastructure platform.

### CTA

Explore strategic partnership, pilot facility, feedstock agreement, or capital participation.

---

## Track 4 - Grant / R&D Video

### Purpose

Explain the AI and robotics retrofit R&D concept for grant partners, universities, research centers, and pilot facilities.

### Target Audience

- robotics partners
- AI/computer vision partners
- university labs
- NSF / EPA / DOE-aligned collaborators
- pilot recycling facilities

### Suggested Length

3 to 5 minutes

### Core Message

Urban Mining is developing an AI- and robotics-enabled retrofit model to help existing recycling facilities automate high-value e-waste identification, sorting, and circularity reporting.

### CTA

Discuss pilot collaboration or letter of support.

# Pictory Production Workflow

## Step 1 - Script

Create a 400 to 800 word script depending on desired length.

## Step 2 - Scene Breakdown

Convert the script into 8 to 15 short scenes.

## Step 3 - Visual Direction

Use visual categories such as:

- data centers
- circuit boards
- servers
- recycling facilities
- robotics arms
- AI interface overlays
- logistics / warehouse footage
- precious metals
- circular economy graphics

## Step 4 - Voiceover

Use either:

- Andrew Ferguson voiceover for investor authority
- Dennis Eng voiceover for GTM / capital formation explanation
- professional neutral narration for general educational videos

## Step 5 - Captions and On-Screen Text

Keep text short.

Examples:

- Feedstock is the ore body
- PCB-rich material is not generic scrap
- Investor capital secures supply
- Grant capital funds automation
- Strategic partners validate deployment

## Step 6 - Compliance Review

Investor-facing videos should be reviewed before distribution.

Review for:

- unapproved return claims
- unsupported technical claims
- misleading ESG claims
- forward-looking statements
- offering language
- accreditation gating requirements

## Step 7 - Distribution

Use videos in:

- personalized email follow-up
- LinkedIn follow-up
- data room introduction
- investor briefing sequence
- strategic partner outreach
- grant partner outreach

# Compliance Guardrails

## Use Video For

- education
- thesis framing
- relationship warming
- explaining the investor journey
- explaining feedstock strategy
- explaining grant and R&D strategy

## Do Not Use Video For

- guaranteeing returns
- replacing offering documents
- bypassing accreditation
- making unsupported claims
- disclosing confidential materials publicly
- presenting projections without counsel review

# Strategic Conclusion

Pictory can become the video education layer of the fundraising system.

It sits between cold outreach and live conversation.

```text
Signal / Prospect Identified
↓
Personalized Outreach
↓
Pictory Video Education
↓
Discovery Call
↓
Qualification / Data Room
↓
Diligence / Commitment
```

Used properly, video should shorten the education cycle, increase trust, and help prospects understand why feedstock control is the central value driver in Urban Mining.
