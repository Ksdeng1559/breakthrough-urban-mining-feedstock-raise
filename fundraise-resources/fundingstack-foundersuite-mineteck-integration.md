# Fundingstack / Foundersuite Integration Brief for MineTeck

## Purpose

This brief captures how Fundingstack / Foundersuite can be used as a fundraising operating platform for MineTeck and the Breakthrough Urban Mining Feedstock Raise.

The goal is not to rebuild Foundersuite. The goal is to integrate it into the capital formation workflow as the fundraising CRM and investor database layer, while using RIOS / Hermes as the intelligence, signal detection, and follow-up orchestration layer.

## Fundingstack Offer Summary

Fundingstack offers month-to-month access at approximately $250/month for its core fundraising platform.

Included functionality:

- Investor database with approximately 164,000 global investors and contact information
- Investor CRM for managing multiple deals, tasks, activities, and follow-ups
- Pitch deck hosting with view tracking
- Investor updates tool for relationship nurturing
- Bulk email tool with follow-up templates
- Virtual data room for confidential information sharing and monitoring, noted as a $150 add-on
- Library of fundraising documents and templates
- Roadmap to warm introductions showing how the company may be connected to investors in the database

## Strategic Assessment

For MineTeck, Fundingstack / Foundersuite should be treated as a capital raise operating platform, not merely software.

The most valuable components are:

1. Investor database
2. Investor CRM
3. Warm introduction mapping
4. Deck tracking
5. Email follow-up workflow
6. Data room support
7. Investor update capability

The highest-value feature for MineTeck is likely the warm introduction roadmap. Capital-intensive industrial opportunities usually benefit more from trusted introductions than cold outreach.

---

## Role Separation

### Fundingstack / Foundersuite Role

Fundingstack should act as the fundraising system of record.

It should track:

- Investors
- Contacts
- Intro paths
- Outreach status
- Meetings
- Follow-up tasks
- Due diligence requests
- Data room access
- Commitments
- Closed capital

### RIOS / Hermes Role

RIOS / Hermes should act as the intelligence and orchestration layer.

It should support:

- Investor signal detection
- Why Now signal analysis
- Investor thesis matching
- Strategic investor prioritization
- Family office intelligence
- Warm introduction prioritization
- Meeting brief generation
- Objection analysis
- Follow-up recommendations
- Capital stack strategy
- Investor update drafting

## Recommended Workflow

### Step 1: Investor Signal Detection

RIOS identifies investors and strategic groups based on relevant signals:

- Critical minerals activity
- Urban mining investment interest
- Mining technology investment
- Circular economy mandates
- ESG and infrastructure capital
- Government funding announcements
- Strategic industry acquisitions
- Resource recovery partnerships
- Mining executives and family office connections

### Step 2: Investor Qualification

Each investor should be scored before being moved into Fundingstack.

Suggested scoring criteria:

- Fit with urban mining
- Fit with critical minerals
- History of resource or infrastructure investing
- Strategic relevance to feedstock or recovery
- Ability to invest $250K+
- Warm introduction potential
- Geographic relevance
- Alignment with MineTeck's stage and risk profile

### Step 3: Push Qualified Targets into Fundingstack

Fundingstack becomes the active fundraising CRM.

Recommended pipeline stages:

1. Identified
2. Qualified
3. Intro path found
4. Intro requested
5. Contacted
6. Meeting scheduled
7. Follow-up sent
8. Due diligence requested
9. Investment committee / partner review
10. Commitment discussion
11. Soft circled
12. Closed
13. Passed / nurture

### Step 4: Use Warm Introduction Roadmap

Use the roadmap to warm introductions before cold outreach wherever possible.

Priority order:

1. Existing advisors
2. Board members
3. Industry executives
4. Strategic partners
5. Current investors
6. Professional service providers
7. Conference contacts
8. Second-degree investor connections

### Step 5: RIOS Generates Investor Battlecards

Before each outreach or meeting, RIOS should generate an investor battlecard including:

- Investor overview
- Investment thesis
- Why MineTeck fits
- Relevant portfolio companies
- Relevant public signals
- Best intro path
- Suggested outreach angle
- Likely objections
- Suggested responses
- Recommended ask
- Follow-up strategy

### Step 6: Manage Follow-Up Discipline

Foundersuite / Fundingstack should manage task tracking and email sequences.

RIOS should add intelligence to follow-up:

- What changed since the last contact
- Which investor concern needs to be addressed
- Which milestone can create urgency
- Which signal creates the next reason to reconnect

## MineTeck $5M Raise Assessment

The question is not whether Fundingstack can raise $5M. Software does not raise capital by itself.

The better question is whether Fundingstack can help MineTeck systematically find and manage enough qualified investor conversations to create a credible path to $5M.

The answer is yes, if MineTeck becomes investor-ready.

### Days 61 to 90: Meeting and DD Conversion

- Run investor meetings

## Bottom-Line Recommendation

Fundingstack / Foundersuite is worth testing for MineTeck.

The $250/month cost is small relative to the potential value of finding even one qualified strategic investor, family office, or resource-sector partner.

Recommended decision:

**Use Fundingstack / Foundersuite as the fundraising CRM and investor database. Use RIOS / Hermes as the investor intelligence, signal detection, and capital formation layer.**

This creates a practical, non-overbuilt path to pursue a $5M raise over 12 months while preserving the long-term RIOS strategy.
