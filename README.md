# Breakthrough Urban Mining Feedstock Raise

## Project Purpose

This repository is the GTM operating system for Breakthrough Management's $5M Urban Mining feedstock acquisition raise.

The immediate purpose of the raise is to secure high-value printed circuit board (PCB), retired server, telecom, and enterprise electronics feedstock for precious metals recovery.

The broader purpose is to build a repeatable feedstock acquisition, investor outreach, grant funding, and strategic partnership engine for the Urban Mining platform.

---

## Core Thesis

In conventional mining, reserves determine value.  
In urban mining, secured feedstock determines value.

Feedstock is the ore body of urban mining.

The business objective is to secure control of high-value electronic waste streams before the market fully reprices PCB-rich and server-derived material.

## 180-Day Execution Framework

The GTM plan runs on a 180-day execution window with formal reviews every 90 days.

### Day 90 Review

Purpose:

- evaluate supplier traction
- review feedstock LOIs
- assess investor engagement
- review data room activity
- decide whether to scale, refine, or reposition

### Day 180 Review

Purpose:

- evaluate capital raised
- evaluate feedstock secured
- review strategic partnerships
- determine next 180-day plan
