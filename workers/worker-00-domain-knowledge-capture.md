# Worker 00: Domain Knowledge Capture

## Purpose

Capture Gilbert Jones' practical feedstock expertise and convert it into structured operating knowledge for RIOS/OpenClaw workers.

This worker should run before automated signal hunting or opportunity scoring.

---

## Inputs

- Interview transcripts
- Voice notes
- Meeting summaries
- Historical examples
- Feedstock photos
- Prior deal notes
- Assay or recovery results
- Buyer/refiner notes

---

## Tasks

1. Extract feedstock categories.
2. Identify high-value and low-value material.
3. Capture pricing rules.
4. Capture recovery assumptions.
5. Capture source types.
6. Capture red flags.
7. Convert examples into reusable decision rules.

---

## Outputs

- Updated feedstock ontology
- Updated recovery economics model
- Risk rules
- Source map
- Worker training examples
- Questions requiring human clarification

---

## Human Review

All extracted rules must be reviewed by Gilbert Jones or Dennis Eng before being used for acquisition decisions.
